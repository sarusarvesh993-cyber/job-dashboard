What an ATS parser does with this specific PDF

Everything above was measured on the uploaded file, not guessed: text extraction, font list, link annotations, image count and the x/y position histogram of 671 words.

Fit estimate weights: requirement coverage 38%, experience vs stated minimum 18%, seniority wording 12%, role-family 12%, domain overlap 8%, tool stack 7%, eligibility/logistics 5%, with hard ceilings when an advert demands years of experience you do not have. Confidence reflects description length, whether years were stated, and posting age — not the quality of your CV.

Job data: public LinkedIn job listings captured on 26 Oct 2026 and each page re-opened to confirm it still showed an active Apply control. No listing dates, salaries or requirements were invented; where the advert did not state something, the field says so.
